# CivicAI Hackathon Demonstration Script (3–5 Minutes)

> “See a problem. Report it. Let AI take it forward.”

This step-by-step presentation script guides you through the live demonstration flow for hackathon judges, municipal evaluators, and stakeholders.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:00 – 0:45: The Problem & The Solution
1. **Open the Homepage**: Show the clean, modern landing page with city selector on **Delhi**.
2. **Key Talking Point**:
   > “In Indian cities, civic grievance portals suffer from 3 bottlenecks: citizens don't know the exact department, complaints lack objective urgency metrics, and issues close without proof. CivicAI solves this with computer vision, an objective priority engine, and verified Before/After resolution evidence.”
3. **Show City Selector**: Switch between Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Pune to demonstrate dynamic city context.
4. **Show Language Switch**: Toggle English ⇄ हिन्दी to showcase indigenous accessibility.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 0:45 – 1:45: Citizen Problem Reporting & AI Analysis
1. Click **“Report a Problem”**.
2. **Upload/Camera**: Drag or select a pothole or garbage dump photo.
3. **Show Client-Side Compression**: Point out the live compression badge (*Original: 3.4 MB → Optimized: 420 KB*). Explain how this enables seamless reporting on 3G/4G networks in congested Indian corridors.
4. **Watch AI Scanner Animation**:
   - High-tech laser overlay scans the defect.
   - Progress indicators show classification, severity scoring, and priority calculation.
5. **Review AI Diagnosis**:
   - Problem: *Large Deep Pothole*
   - Category: *Road Infrastructure*
   - AI Confidence: *94%*
   - Priority Score: *93/100 (P1 — Critical)*
   - Open the **“Why P1?”** card to demonstrate the transparent mathematical breakdown (High safety risk to two-wheelers + major arterial road).
6. **Location & Duplicate Detection**:
   - Location automatically pins to the nearest landmark.
   - If reporting near Connaught Place, point out the **Duplicate Alert Banner** preventing redundant tickets.
7. Click **“Submit Report”**.
8. **Show Confirmation Screen**: Instant report ID generated (e.g. `CA-2026-00124`) with priority index P1.

---

## ⏱️ Minute 1:45 – 2:45: Interactive Map & Citizen Tracking
1. Click **“Track Live Timeline”**: Show the 6-stage lifecycle (*Reported → AI Analysis → Verified → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved*).
2. Click **“Explore Issues”**:
   - Toggle to **Interactive Map**: Show color-coded priority pins across the city (Red for P1, Orange for P2, Yellow for P3, Blue for P4).
   - Click a marker to open the quick-dispatch popup.
3. Click **“My Reports”**: Show the citizen's personal dashboard with live status cards (Pending, In Progress, Resolved).

---

## ⏱️ Minute 2:45 – 4:00: Municipal Operations & Before/After Proof
1. **Switch Role to Admin**: In the top navbar, switch role to **“MCD / City Admin”** with one click.
2. Navigate to **“Civic Operations”** (Admin Dashboard):
   - Review the Delhi Civic Operations triage board.
   - Filter by **P1 Critical** tickets.
3. **Lifecycle Transition**:
   - Click **Wrench Icon** on an unassigned issue.
   - Assign to *“Inspector Rajesh Verma (PWD Central Division)”* and mark **“In Progress”**.
4. **Upload Resolution Proof (The Killer Feature)**:
   - Click **“Resolve”** on the ticket.
   - Upload the **“After”** photo showing repaired asphalt or cleared pavement.
   - Enter completion note: *“Crater patched with bitumen mix, lane rolled and swept.”*
   - Click **“Mark Incident Resolved”**.
5. **Inspect the Resolved Issue**:
   - Open the ticket detail page.
   - Display the **Before/After Split Viewer**: Show the original problem next to the verified repair photo!
   - Highlight: *“This turns a complaint portal into an accountable civic audit system.”*

---

## ⏱️ Minute 4:00 – 4:30: Closing Pitch
- **Scalability**: Works for cities (MCD, BMC), college campuses (BRCM), RWAs, and tech parks.
- **Lightweight Architecture**: <15MB source code, 0 heavy local models, deployable anywhere in minutes.
